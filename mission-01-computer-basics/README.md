# Mission 1 – Know Your Computer

# Task 1.1 – IPOS for Taking and Sharing a Photo
1. Input: The process begins with the camera, which acts as an input device. It captures an image of a particular moment and converts it into digital data that can be understood by the computer or smartphone.
2. Processing: Once the photograph is captured, the processing unit, such as the CPU or GPU, performs the necessary operations on the image data. These operations may include image enhancement, colour correction, and compression to prepare the photograph for display or sharing.
3. Output: After processing, the photograph is displayed on the device's screen or monitor. This allows the user to view the captured image and check its quality.
4. Storage: The photograph is temporarily handled in RAM while the device processes it. For long-term storage, it is saved in non-volatile storage devices such as internal SSDs or flash memory. This allows the user to access, edit, and share the photograph even after the device is switched off.

# Task 1.3 – Computer Systems Around You
1. ATM (Automated Teller Machine)
- Main purpose: Allows users to withdraw cash and perform banking transactions.
- Input: ATM card.
- Output: Cash.
2. Washing Machine
- Main purpose: Washes clothes automatically.
- Input: Setting of the water level.
- Output: Washed clothes.
3. Traffic Signal System
- Main purpose: Controls the movement of vehicles and pedestrians to ensure smooth and safe traffic flow.
- Input: Detection of vehicles through sensors.
- Output: Red, yellow, and green lights.
